SEC.03 // PROCESS

How it all happens.

STEP 01

You tell me the problem

A call or a message. What's the business, who's the customer, what should the site actually do for you.

STEP 02

I sketch it in Figma

Layout and structure first, before any code. You see it and say yes, or tell me what's off.

STEP 03

I build it

Real code, deployed to a staging link so you can check it on your own phone as it comes together.

STEP 04

You launch, I stay on call

Site goes live on your domain. First month of small fixes is on me — no separate invoice for typos.
